I'm a PhD candidate in Philosophy at the University of Reading (funded by the South, West & Wales Doctoral Training Partnership), supervised by Luke Elson (Reading) and Lee Walters (Southampton).

I did an MA in Philosophy at the Università della Svizzera italiana (USI), supervised by Martine Nida-Rümelin (Fribourg), and an MA in Philosophy at the University of Parma. I did my BA in Philosophical and Educational Sciences at the University of Ferrara.

My research is mainly in metaphysics — especially personal identity and persistence, and in particular perdurantism — with related interests in ethics and epistemology. Much of my recent work concerns the "Personite Problem" and how theories of persistence bear on moral responsibility and value.
